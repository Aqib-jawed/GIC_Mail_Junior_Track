GITAM x Bower Innovation Challenge 2026

Dear [First Name],

Why do great school ideas never leave the notebook? Rarely for lack of talent. Almost always for one of four reasons.

  1. No one to ask. Nobody experienced is questioning the idea.
  2. No practice presenting. The idea is clear in your head and muddled when you explain it.
  3. No audience. Nobody outside the classroom ever hears it.
  4. No deadline. Without one, "someday" becomes never.

GIC is built to remove each gap.

  • A mentor: every advancing team gets a personal mentor during the online bootcamp, 9-11 November.
  • Practice: a Round 2 deck, a 20-minute online semi-final pitch on 21 November and, for finalists, a live stage in Hyderabad.
  • An audience and a programme: every Junior finalist receives access to the LEAD Program by Bower School of Entrepreneurship.
  • A deadline: a fixed calendar and a Pitch Deck Template to download from the website.

For parents: teamwork, research and presenting are what every team practises along the way.

An idea needs a deadline to become a project. This one is 31 October, 11:59 PM IST.
